# Kaeno — Image to WebP Converter

**Lighter Format. Same Visual Quality.**

Kaeno is a minimal, privacy-focused image converter that transforms PNG and JPG images into WebP format. Designed with simplicity and performance in mind, Kaeno makes image conversion easy on both desktop and mobile devices.

Designed by **Mohit RW** and brought to life using Antigravity IDE, Kaeno combines original UI design with functional development to deliver a lightweight, accessible image conversion experience.

## ✨ Features

- 🖼️ **Image Conversion** — Convert PNG, JPG, and JPEG images into WebP format.
- 🔒 **Privacy First** — Images are processed locally in your browser. Your files are not uploaded to any server.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive** — Use Kaeno on desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and mobile devices.
- 📦 **Offline PWA** — Install Kaeno as a Progressive Web App and use it offline whenever you need to convert images.
- 📂 **Drag & Drop** — Easily add images by dragging files into the upload area or selecting them from your device.
- 📊 **File Size Comparison** — View original and converted file sizes to see how much space you save.
- ⚡ **Fast Conversion** — Convert images directly in your browser without relying on a remote conversion server.
- 🎨 **Custom UI Design** — An original, minimal interface with a dark aesthetic and custom branding.
- 💻 **Cross-Device Experience** — Designed to provide a consistent experience across different screen sizes.

## 🔐 Your Files Stay Private

**Your images stay on your device.**

Kaeno processes images locally in your browser instead of uploading them to an external conversion server.

- No image uploads to a remote conversion server.
- Your original files remain under your control.
- Image conversion works locally in the browser.
- Once installed and properly cached, Kaeno can be used offline without an internet connection.

Your privacy is a core part of Kaeno's design.

## 📲 Install Kaeno as a PWA

Kaeno supports installation as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**, allowing you to use it like a regular application.

1. Open the Kaeno website in a supported browser.
2. Choose the browser's **Install App** option, if available.
3. Install Kaeno on your device.
4. Open the installed application whenever you need it.
5. Convert your images, even when you're offline, once the app's required resources have been cached.

PWA installation and offline availability depend on browser support and successful caching of the application's required resources.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Visit the [Kaeno website](https://mohitrw.github.io/kaeno/).
2. Drag and drop your PNG, JPG, or JPEG images into the upload area, or select them from your device.
3. Review the selected files and their original sizes.
4. Click the conversion button to convert your images to WebP.
5. Review the converted files and their new sizes.
6. Download your converted images.

You can also install Kaeno as a PWA for convenient access and offline use.
